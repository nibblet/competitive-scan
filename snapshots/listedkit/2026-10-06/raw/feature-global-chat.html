Questions

Global chat, answered.

Common questions about the one Ava chat that answers across every deal, how it differs from a single-deal chat, and how it stays scoped to what you are allowed to see.

What is global chat in ListedKit?

Global chat is the one place in ListedKit where Ava answers across every deal you are part of at once. Every other Ava chat is scoped to a single deal file. Global chat is not: you can ask what is outstanding across all your deals, how your closings are tracking, or what is due this week, and Ava answers from your actual data across the whole account.

How is global chat different from asking Ava inside a deal?

Inside a deal, Ava answers about that one file. Global chat answers across every deal you are part of. When you need the cross-deal picture, the kind of rollup you would otherwise rebuild in a spreadsheet, global chat is where you ask for it in one question.

Can everyone on the team see every deal in global chat?

No. Global chat is permission-scoped. An admin gets answers across the whole team's deals. An agent gets answers across their own deals only. Ava never returns a deal you are not allowed to see, so the same question gives each person an answer scoped to their access.

What kinds of questions can I ask in global chat?

Anything that spans more than one deal. "What is outstanding across my 40 deals?" "Which files close this week?" "How are closings across the account performing?" "What is stalled and waiting on me?" Ava reads across your deals and answers from your real data, so you get the whole picture without opening each file.
