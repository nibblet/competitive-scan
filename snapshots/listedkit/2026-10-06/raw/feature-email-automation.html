How it works

Three ways Ava handles email

Not one mode. Not a generic email writer. Three distinct workflows, all from real deal context.

01

Auto-reply

Ava reads your incoming emails and drafts a reply using deal context, the prior thread, and what it knows needs to happen next. You review and send. Nothing leaves without you.

02

On-demand

Any email, any situation. Tell Ava what you need: "congrats, buyer is in escrow, send timeline," "explain the inspection contingency to the buyer," "update everyone that closing moved to Friday." It drafts it with the real details from your file.

03

Scheduled reminders

You schedule the reminders and Ava sends them at the time you set: inspection confirmation 48 hours out, contingency follow-up 3 days before expiration, closing checklist a week before close. Set it once per deal or ask Ava to schedule from the contract, and nothing goes out that you did not put on the calendar.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how Ava drafts, schedules, and sends transaction email from your Gmail or Outlook, and where you stay in control of every send.

Will my clients know AI wrote the email?

No. Ava sends emails directly from your Gmail or Outlook account. Recipients see your name, your email address, and your signature. There is zero AI branding anywhere in the email. Your clients and agents will never know the difference.

How does Ava know the details of my transaction when drafting emails?

Ava has already read your contract. When you prompt an email like 'send the buyer's agent an update on the inspection timeline,' Ava already knows the agent's name, the property address, the inspection deadline, and the current status. The email goes out with real details, not placeholders you have to fill in.

Can I still use my own email templates?

Yes. You can save your best messages as reusable templates with smart placeholders that fill in automatically from each transaction's data. You can also import existing emails by dragging files or copy-pasting. Ava's AI-powered search finds the right template instantly, and you can edit anything before it sends.

What if Ava drafts something I don't like?

You always review before anything sends. Ava drafts the email and shows it to you. You can edit, rewrite, or prompt Ava to adjust the tone or content. No draft leaves your inbox without your approval.

How does real estate email automation work?

Ava reads your contract when a new deal opens and stores all the deal context: parties, property address, deadlines, and contingency terms. When an email arrives that relates to a deal, Ava can draft a reply using that context. When you need an email on demand, you give Ava a plain-language instruction and it writes it using the real details from your file. For scheduled reminders, you tell Ava which deadline to send around and who should receive it, and the reminder you scheduled goes out to the right parties at the time you set.

Can Ava send scheduled reminder emails automatically?

Yes. You can schedule Ava to send reminder emails ahead of specific deadlines: inspection confirmation 48 hours out, contingency follow-up 3 days before expiration, closing checklist a week before close. Set it per deal or ask Ava to schedule reminders directly from the contract. Reminders go out from your Gmail or Outlook at the time you specify.

Does Ava reply to incoming emails automatically?

Ava reads incoming emails and drafts replies for your review. It does not send automatically without your approval. When an email arrives on a deal, Ava drafts a reply based on the deal file, the thread history, and what needs to happen next. You review the draft and send. You stay in control of what leaves your inbox.
