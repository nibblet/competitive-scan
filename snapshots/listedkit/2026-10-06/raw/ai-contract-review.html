Contract reading, answered.

Common questions about how Ava reads purchase agreements, what it extracts, and how per-contract pricing compares to monthly TC software.

How is ListedKit AI different from other transaction coordinator software?

ListedKit AI is the only transaction coordinator software with true AI contract reading. Other platforms like Dotloop, SkySlope, and Paperless Pipeline require you to manually enter contract data into templates. ListedKit AI reads the actual purchase agreement, extracts every detail, and builds the full transaction in under 60 seconds with no data entry required.

Does ListedKit AI work with contracts from my state?

Yes. ListedKit AI reads purchase agreements from all 50 states with zero pre-setup. There are no state-specific templates to configure. Whether you work with California PRDS forms, Texas TREC contracts, New York attorney-state agreements, or any other format, Ava reads them in real time and calculates deadlines according to each state's rules.

Can ListedKit AI read handwritten contracts and amendments?

Yes. ListedKit AI reads handwritten contracts, amendments, and counteroffers with human-level accuracy. It also follows logic across multiple counteroffers to find the final agreed terms, so you don't have to cross-reference documents manually.

How does ListedKit AI handle complex deadline calculations?

ListedKit AI converts relative deadlines like '7 business days before closing' or '5 calendar days after acceptance' into actual calendar dates. It accounts for weekends, federal holidays, and state-specific business day rules automatically. These deadlines sync directly to Google Calendar or Outlook in one click.

How much does ListedKit AI cost compared to other TC software?

ListedKit AI charges $14.99 per contract intake with no monthly subscription. Your first intake is completely free. Most competing TC platforms charge a flat monthly subscription no matter how many transactions you actually run that month. For TCs with variable volume, usage-based pricing means you never pay for months when business is slow.
