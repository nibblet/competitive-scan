Questions

Pricing, answered.

What counts as a credit?

Each credit covers one transaction stage: one to create a listing, and one to create a sale once it goes under contract.

Is there really no subscription?

None. No contracts, no monthly fees. You buy credits when you need them, and they never expire.

Is my first transaction really free?

Yes. Your first intake is 100% free, no credit card, no commitment. Run a real deal through Ava before you buy a single credit.

How do volume discounts work?

Buy credits in packages of 5, 10, 25, or 50 and the per-credit rate drops, up to 27% off. Need more than 50? Schedule a volume call for custom pricing.

Do you charge per team member?

No. Invite unlimited team members. One account, one shared credit pool, no per-seat fees.

Is Ava Sign included?

Ava Sign is a separate add-on at $29.99 per user per month, free for the first 30 days. One license covers unlimited signature requests, and recipients always sign for free.
