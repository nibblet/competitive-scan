Questions

Ava Sign, answered.

What is Ava Sign?

Ava Sign is e-signature built right into ListedKit. Ava already has the parties, the property, and the documents from the deal, so preparing a signature request is one step, not a blank form you rebuild every time. Add your fields, choose who signs, and send. Then Ava tracks who has and has not signed and files the executed copy back on the deal automatically.

How is Ava Sign different from a standalone e-signature tool?

Most e-sign tools start you from scratch: type the names, drag the fields, then chase down who signed. Ava Sign starts from your deal. It fills the document in with the parties and property it already knows, sends it for signature, and tracks every name until it is done. Ava fills the document in from the deal and tracks the signatures, which saves real time across a full pipeline.

What documents does Ava Sign cover?

Ava Sign covers your listing and closing paperwork: listing agreements, agency and representation disclosures, seller property disclosures, addenda, amendments, contingency removals, repair agreements, and documents like them. It does not do the offer and counteroffer exchange between buyer and seller.

How much does Ava Sign cost?

Ava Sign is free for 30 days, then $29.99 per user. No card to start. One license covers unlimited signature requests, so a busy month costs the same as a slow one.

Do the people I send documents to need an account?

No. Recipients sign for free with no account and no license of their own. The per-user license is only for the person on your team sending the request.

Does it cost anything to prepare a document?

No. Preparing a document is free. Adding fields and choosing who signs costs nothing. Only sending it needs the trial or a license.

Is a signature from Ava Sign legally valid and binding?

Yes. A signature collected through Ava Sign is legally valid and binding, the same as any compliant electronic signature (this excludes documents that do not fall under ESIGN and UETA).

Is Ava Sign compliant with ESIGN and UETA e-signature requirements?

Yes. Ava Sign meets the ESIGN and UETA requirements for electronic signatures.
