The First 3 Transactions Framework

Think of your first 3 transactions as training Ava, not learning a new tool.

1

Transaction 1: Teach Ava Your Basics

What to do:

Run a typical transaction through the system. Review every field Ava extracts. Delete fields you don't need. Edit anything that's wrong. Add any fields it missed.

Why it matters:

Ava watches what you keep, delete, and change. This becomes its baseline for how you work.

2

Transaction 2: Show Ava Variety

What to do:

Pick a different type of deal - if #1 was a purchase, do a listing. Or try a different property type (condo vs. single family). Review and correct just like transaction 1.

Why it matters:

Ava learns that different deals need different approaches. It won't apply purchase rules to a listing.

3

Transaction 3: Handle an Edge Case

What to do:

Run something unusual - a different brokerage's forms, a different state, or a deal with unique terms. Take extra time reviewing.

Why it matters:

Ava learns to handle exceptions. After this, it's seen enough variety to work independently.

After Transaction 3, Ava will:

Suggest the right template based on transaction details
Auto-draft more accurate emails (it knows when to use which template)
Require less review time - it's learned your patterns
Let you shift from 'trainer' to 'reviewer'

Your First Transaction

This is where you teach Ava how you work. Take your time.

1

Upload Your Contract

  1. Click "New Transaction" (top left)
  2. Upload your purchase agreement OR listing agreement
  3. Include all addendums, riders, and disclosures
  4. Don't worry about splitting - upload files as-is
2

Confirm the Execution Date

Ava extracts the execution date first. This is the foundation for all timeline calculations.

Tip:Click on the date to see exactly where it pulled it from (source verification)
3

Review Transaction Details

Ava extracts: property address, HOA info, parties, financing details, and contingencies.

VerifyClick to see source
EditClick pencil to fix errors
DeleteOptionally check "never extract again"
AddClick "Add field" for missing info

Your edits during these first transactions are training data.

4

Confirm Transaction Timeline

Ava calculates all deadlines, including relative dates like "7 business days before closing."

  • • Click on dates to verify source and calculation
  • • Pay extra attention to contingency deadlines
  • • Edit anything that looks off
5

Apply Your Compliance Checklist

Generate with Ava

Upload your template

Have a brokerage-required or proven list

Apply Your Task Template

Same process as the compliance checklist.

Look for the "Email Task" tag: Tasks with this tag mean Ava will auto-draft on the due date.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ava AI and how does it help transaction coordinators?

Ava is an AI transaction assistant built into ListedKit that reads your real estate contracts, extracts key dates and terms, creates task timelines, drafts emails, syncs with Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar, and learns your workflow over time. Unlike traditional TC software, Ava adapts to how you work rather than forcing you to adapt to a rigid system.

How long does it take to set up ListedKit and train Ava?

Initial setup takes about 15-30 minutes to connect your Google or Microsoft integrations and upload templates. Ava learns your preferences through your first 3 transactions - by transaction 4, it works on auto-pilot with minimal corrections needed. Most users see significant time savings within their first week.

Can Ava read any type of real estate contract?

Yes, Ava can read any state's purchase agreement, listing agreement, or addendum in real-time with no pre-setup required. It handles handwritten contracts with high accuracy, follows logic across multiple counteroffers, and calculates complex timelines like '7 business days before closing' automatically.

How does Ava's email integration work?

Once you connect Gmail or Outlook, Ava can draft and send emails directly from your inbox - no AI branding appears. It uses your uploaded templates and transaction details to create personalized emails. You can give its simple prompts like 'email the buyer about earnest money' and it drafts a complete message with names, amounts, and dates.

What integrations does ListedKit support?

ListedKit integrates with Follow Up Boss for importing contacts directly into transactions, Gmail and Outlook for sending transaction emails, and Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar for syncing deadlines and events with automatic invites to relevant parties.

How does the compliance scanning feature work?

Click the shield icon on any document and Ava checks for missing signatures, information mismatches against transaction data, and missing information. This 30-second scan helps catch errors before documents go to the broker or closing.

Can multiple team members use ListedKit on the same transactions?

Yes, ListedKit supports team collaboration. Multiple team members can work with Ava simultaneously on the same transactions. The system recognizes repeat contacts and remembers preferences, and supports custom permission levels for assistants, agents, and admins.
