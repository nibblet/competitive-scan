How it works

Simple prompt, complete coordination

Tell Ava what you need and it handles the scheduling, invites, and updates.

01

Tell Ava what to schedule

One message names the event, the time, and the parties. "Schedule the final walkthrough for next Tuesday at 2pm, invite the buyer, listing agent, and inspector" is all Ava needs to start coordinating.

02

Ava creates the calendar events

It adds every deadline you asked for to Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar as real events, on the dates the contract sets, and updates the transaction timeline to match.

03

Invites reach the parties you name

Buyer, listing agent, inspector, lender: every party you tell Ava to include receives a calendar invite and the timeline view that goes with it. No individual emails for you to write.

04

Dates change, the timeline follows

When an amendment moves a deadline, Ava updates the shared timeline, so every party you invited is looking at the current schedule instead of last week's dates.
