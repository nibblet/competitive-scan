Questions

Compliance scanning, answered.

Common questions about what Ava checks, when the scan runs, and how it works alongside the compliance system your brokerage requires.

What software scans real estate transaction documents for compliance issues?

Ava scans real estate transaction documents for four kinds of problems: missing signatures and initials, blank or incomplete information, information mismatches between documents, and referenced documents that are missing from the file. Each finding is graded by severity and includes a suggested action. The scan supports a brokerage's review process; it does not replace required supervision, legal advice, or the brokerage's system of record.

When does the compliance scan run?

At intake and throughout the deal. Ava scans documents as they come in, so compliance issues surface early rather than at closing. As new documents arrive, they're scanned too, and the compliance picture updates. You can also run a scan on demand whenever you want a fresh check on a file.

Does Ava replace my brokerage's compliance system?

No, and it isn't meant to. Ava is the layer that catches issues before your file goes to the system your brokerage mandates. It works inside your current process: give Ava your brokerage's compliance checklist and it tracks the file against those rules from day one, then you submit the finished file to your compliance system with nothing missing.

Does the compliance scan work in my state?

Yes. Ava reads contracts and documents from any state with no pre-setup or templates. It checks the documents you actually have against the parties and terms in the deal, and against your brokerage's checklist if you give it one, so it works the same whether you're in a California PRDS deal or a Texas TREC deal.

How much does the compliance scan cost?

Compliance scanning is part of ListedKit AI, which is $14.99 per transaction intake with no monthly subscription. Your first intake is free. You're not buying a separate compliance product on top of a monthly platform fee, the scan runs on the same deals you're already coordinating.
