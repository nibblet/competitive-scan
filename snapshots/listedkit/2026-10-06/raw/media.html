Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is ListedKit AI?

ListedKit AI is a transaction management platform for real estate professionals. Ava, the AI engine, reads incoming emails and contracts in real time, matches every message to the right deal, extracts dates and parties, and manages timelines from intake to closing without pre-setup or templates.

What does Ava do in a real estate transaction?

Ava reads the contract at intake, extracts every date, party, and contingency, then watches the inbox so every email is filed to the right deal automatically. It tracks deadlines across all 50 states and surfaces what needs attention next, so nothing falls through.

How is AI changing transaction coordination in real estate?

AI moves transaction coordination away from template-based systems where a human builds a checklist for each deal. Tools like Ava read the actual contract at intake, build the timeline automatically, monitor the inbox for related emails, and surface deadlines before they are missed. Teams handle more deals without adding headcount.

What part of the deal does nobody want to touch?

The administrative layer: reading the contract line by line, tracking every deadline across 40+ active files, filing emails to the right deal, and making sure nothing slips. That is exactly what Ava handles, which is what the Icons of Real Estate episode is about.

Does ListedKit AI work for solo agents or only teams?

Both. Solo agents use it to handle their own transaction administration without hiring a TC. Teams and brokerages use it to give a single TC the capacity to manage more files without burning out. Pricing is per transaction, so it scales with volume in both directions.
