How the team dashboard works

Open the team dashboard

The team dashboard is the admin-only view of the whole team. Open it and the question it answers is right there: is each teammate keeping up? See the team by teammate, by task, or by transaction, no file-by-file digging.

Questions

Team dashboard, answered.

Common questions about the admin-only team dashboard, who can see it, and how it surfaces stalled deals across your whole team.

What is the team dashboard in ListedKit?

The team dashboard is an admin-only view of whether each teammate is keeping up. For every agent and TC it shows their active transactions, what is overdue, and how many tasks they completed this week, and you can view the team by teammate, by task, or by transaction. Drill into anyone to see their overdue tasks and remind them, without opening every file or asking for a status update.

Who can see the team dashboard?

The team dashboard is admin-only. Team leads, brokers, and TC business owners who administer the account see the whole team's deals and activity. Agents and individual TCs work in their own deals and do not see the team-wide view.

How does the team dashboard help me catch a teammate falling behind?

It shows each teammate's overdue count right next to their active load and what they completed this week, so the person falling behind stands out. Open them to see exactly which tasks are overdue, down to the property and how many days late, then send a reminder on one or on all of them, before it becomes a closing problem.

Do I have to ask my team for status updates?

No. That is the point of the dashboard. It shows the whole team's deal status without anyone reporting in. You see who is working what and where deals stand without pulling people off their work to answer a status question.
