Frequently Asked Questions

Is there really a free plan?

Yes! Our free plan includes 2 active deals, 10 free skip traces, and core features like Driving for Dollars, AI deal scoring, and the full mobile app. No credit card required, no time limit.

Can I change plans anytime?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. When upgrading, you get immediate access to new features. When downgrading, changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

What happens if I exceed my limits?

We will notify you when you are approaching limits. You can upgrade your plan or purchase additional capacity at competitive overage rates.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes! Annual billing saves you 20%. Pro is $950/yr (~$79/mo) and Team is $1,910/yr (~$159/mo). Toggle annual billing on the upgrade page.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use bank-level encryption, SOC 2 compliant infrastructure, and never share your data with third parties.
