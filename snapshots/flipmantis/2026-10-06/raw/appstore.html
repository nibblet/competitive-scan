Find deals while driving. Analyze properties with AI. Manage your pipeline on the go. FlipMantis is the real estate investor's secret weapon. FlipMantis is the all-in-one mobile app for real estate investors who want to find, analyze, and close more deals - faster. Whether you're flipping houses, building a rental portfolio, or wholesaling, FlipMantis puts institutional-grade tools in your pocket. DRIVING FOR DOLLARS Turn every drive into deal flow. Our GPS-powered route tracking automatically logs properties as you drive through neighborhoods. Spot a distressed property? Tap to capture it - even offline. Your leads sync when you're back online. AI-POWERED ANALYSIS Stop guessing. Our AI analyzes every property against your custom buy box criteria and scores it instantly. Get ARV estimates, rehab projections, and profit analysis - all powered by real market data. DEAL PIPELINE Track every deal from lead to close with our visual pipeline. Drag-and-drop deals through stages, set follow-up reminders, and never let an opportunity slip through the cracks. SKIP TRACE Find property owners instantly. Our integrated skip trace pulls phone numbers and emails so you can make contact the same day you find a property. KEY FEATURES - Driving for Dollars with GPS tracking - AI property scoring and buy box matching - 10-stage deal pipeline management - Integrated skip trace lookups - Property search with instant valuations - Swipe-to-triage lead qualification - Offline mode - work without signal - Voice notes with AI transcription - Comp analysis with market data - Photo capture for property evaluation BUILT FOR INVESTORS FlipMantis was built by investors, for investors. We know the hustle. We know the margins matter. That's why we built the tool we wished existed. Whether you're doing 2 deals a year or 20 deals a month, FlipMantis scales with you.
