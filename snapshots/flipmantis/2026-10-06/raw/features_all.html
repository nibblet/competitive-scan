Common Questions

What features are included in FlipMantis?
FlipMantis includes 166 features across 11 categories: Lead Generation, Driving for Dollars, Communication (power dialer, AI voice agents, unified inbox), CRM & Pipeline, Automation, Intelligence & AI (Mantis Score, buy box matching), Rehab Management, Calendar & Scheduling, Reporting & Analytics, Mobile Exclusive features, and Admin & Enterprise tools.
Does FlipMantis have skip tracing?
Yes. FlipMantis has built-in skip tracing. Find owner phone numbers, emails, and mailing addresses instantly. Includes bulk skip tracing, litigator detection, and DNC compliance checking.
Can I use FlipMantis on mobile?
Yes. FlipMantis has native mobile apps for iOS and Android. 95 features work on mobile, including driving for dollars with GPS tracking, one-tap property capture, offline mode, push notifications, and click-to-call.
Does FlipMantis have AI calling?
Yes. FlipMantis includes AI Voice Agents that call leads 24/7, qualify sellers, book appointments, and extract motivation signals. It also includes call quality scoring across 7 dimensions and sentiment tracking.
What CRM features does FlipMantis have?
FlipMantis has a purpose-built CRM with 7 pipeline types (acquisition, disposition, wholesale, rehab, lending, rental, custom), 16 contact types, kanban boards, offer management, document vault, activity timeline, and full stage history tracking.
How much does FlipMantis cost?
FlipMantis starts at $99/month for the Pro plan with all 166 features included. There is a free tier to get started and no credit card required for the trial.
What is the Mantis Score?
The Mantis Score is an AI-powered motivation score from 0-100 that ranks leads into Hot (70+), Warm (40-69), and Cold (under 40) lanes. It analyzes property data, distress signals, and communication history to prioritize your outreach.
How does FlipMantis compare to PropStream?
PropStream is list-building only. FlipMantis has the same list building plus skip tracing, power dialer, AI voice agents, CRM, sequences, rehab tracking, and 150+ more features. One subscription instead of five.
Is FlipMantis better than BatchLeads?
BatchLeads focuses on skip tracing and basic outreach. FlipMantis has skip tracing plus AI voice agents, 7-pipeline CRM, visual sequence builder, rehab management, stakeholder portals, and full analytics. More depth at a similar price.
Can FlipMantis replace my current tool stack?
Most investors replace 4-6 tools with FlipMantis: PropStream (lists), Mojo (dialer), BatchLeads (skip trace), ActiveCampaign (sequences), Calendly (booking), Podio or spreadsheets (CRM), and BuilderTrend (rehab). All in one platform.
How does FlipMantis compare to REI/kit or Carrot?
REI/kit and Carrot are website builders with basic CRM. FlipMantis is a full operating system: list building, skip tracing, AI calling, 7-pipeline CRM, automation sequences, rehab tracking, and analytics. Different category.
Does FlipMantis work for wholesalers?
Yes. FlipMantis was built for wholesalers first. It has driving for dollars, skip tracing, power dialer, AI voice agents, disposition pipeline, cash buyer lists, and assignment tracking. Everything wholesalers need.
Can teams use FlipMantis?
Yes. FlipMantis has role-based access, team leaderboards, shared pipelines, route sharing for D4D, and activity tracking. Enterprise plans include SSO, custom branding, and dedicated support.
Does FlipMantis have automation?
Yes. FlipMantis has a visual sequence builder with multi-channel steps (call, text, email, task), conditional branching, 10+ triggers, AI lead scoring, and auto-task creation. Like ActiveCampaign but built for real estate.
What makes FlipMantis different from other real estate software?
Three things: AI Voice Agents that call leads for you, the Pass Pile Watcher that tracks deals you skipped (so you learn from misses), and the Mantis Score that prioritizes leads automatically. No other platform has all three.
