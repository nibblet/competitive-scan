Data Fields Included

Comprehensive investor data with everything you need for outreach and analysis.

Investor Profile

  • Investor name (individual or entity)
  • Entity type (LLC, Trust, Corporation, Individual)
  • True owner identification (entity resolution)
  • Years active in market
  • Investor classification (landlord, flipper, corporate)

Portfolio Data

  • Total properties owned
  • Property types (SFR, multi-family, commercial)
  • Geographic footprint (states, metros, ZIP codes)
  • Estimated portfolio value
  • Acquisition history with dates and prices

Activity Metrics

  • Acquisitions in last 12 months
  • Dispositions in last 12 months
  • Average hold period
  • Typical acquisition price range
  • Financing patterns (cash vs. financed)

Contact Information

  • Mailing address
  • Phone number (when available)
  • Email address (when available)
  • Registered agent information

Full data dictionary with field definitions, data types, and coverage rates available upon request.

Who Uses This Data

Real estate professionals across the industry leverage active investor data for outreach and market intelligence.

Wholesalers & Acquisitions

Build targeted buyer lists segmented by investment strategy, price range, and geographic focus. Know exactly who's buying in your market.

Direct Mail Campaigns

Run acquisition campaigns to active investors at scale. Segment by portfolio size, activity level, or investment type.

Private Lenders

Identify qualified borrowers with proven track records. Target investors based on acquisition velocity and deal volume.

Service Providers

Title companies, insurance agents, and contractors can target active investors with relevant services.

Market Research

Analyze investor activity patterns, market saturation, and competitive dynamics. Track institutional vs. individual buyer share.

M&A and Partnerships

Identify potential acquisition targets or JV partners. Find operators looking to scale or exit their portfolios.
