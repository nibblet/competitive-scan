Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is your data?

Our data is compiled from public records and quality-checked by our team. Coverage and contact availability vary by market and source, so profiles should be used as directional market intelligence for outreach and analysis.

How often is the data updated?

Our database is updated daily (Monday-Friday) with a typical lag of 2-3 weeks from recording date, ensuring you have access to the most current market intelligence available.

What information is available about investors?

For each investor, we provide portfolio details such as properties owned, purchase dates, prices, acquisition patterns, market focus, estimated portfolio value, and contact-enriched profile fields.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. We do not lock you into long-term contracts. Monthly subscriptions can be canceled before the next billing cycle, and annual subscriptions can receive a prorated refund for unused months.
