Private Lender Radar

Generate borrower pipeline from daily-updated county deed and mortgage filings, assessor data, entity records, and lender signals. Track 40,000+ private lenders, verify deal activity, and prioritize outreach by market.

Key Features

  • Nationwide coverage with daily updates of deed, mortgage, assessor, and entity data
  • Verify borrower activity in target markets with contact-enriched borrower data
  • Track competitor activity and find borrowers who have used multiple lenders
  • Filter by location down to ZIP code level, and export targeted lists for direct outreach

Investor Edge

Track active buyers, compare market signals, analyze investment opportunities, and build targeted buyer lists from public-record purchase activity for wholesaling, acquisitions, and dispositions.

Key Features

  • Detailed buyer profiles with portfolio analysis and contact information where available
  • Interactive heat maps showing active investor purchases and market trends
  • Buyer-list and deal-review workflows for wholesalers, investors, and operators
  • Customizable deal flow management with targeted opportunity alerts
