What changes with TMO Analytics

Market activity and actual loan performance, in one progression

Private Lender Radar provides public-record intelligence about lenders, borrowers, properties, and market activity. TMO Analytics adds anonymized loan performance data from loans serviced on the TMO platform, enriched with public records and SFR Analytics data.

Private Lender Radar

Shows what is happening in the market.

Find active borrowers, understand lender relationships, compare property-type activity, and move prospecting data into your team's existing workflows.

TMO Analytics

Shows how those loans are performing.

Analyze aggregated delinquency rates by geography, LTV, FICO band, property type, purchase price, and borrower experience, with address-level local benchmarks and nationwide comparisons.

Granular insight without individual exposure

TMO Analytics presents aggregated and anonymized market-level trends and risk indicators. It does not expose individual TMO loans, borrowers, or personally identifiable information.

Designed for private-lending owners and executives, credit and risk leaders, underwriters, asset managers, originations and pricing teams, and institutional loan buyers evaluating pools against broader market benchmarks.

Pricing questions, answered

Are these plans available month to month?

Private Lender Radar Local, Private Lender Radar Regional, and TMO Analytics National are annual agreements.

What geographic coverage does the Local plan include?

Private Lender Radar Local includes one state. Additional state coverage is available for $2,000/year per state.

Are seats priced separately?

No. Every plan includes unlimited team seats.

Do The Mortgage Office customers receive bundled pricing?

Current TMO customers may qualify for bundled pricing when adding SFR Analytics products. Final pricing depends on product mix and rollout scope. The final bundle is confirmed in your quote rather than applied as a blanket percentage to every customer.

How do contact unlocks and API and MCP usage credits work?

A contact information unlock gives your team access to the available contact information for that record and counts against the plan's monthly allowance. API and MCP usage credits cover programmatic data queries and supported AI-tool workflows. Private Lender Radar Regional includes unlimited contact unlocks, while TMO Analytics National includes unlimited contact unlocks and usage credits.

What is included in TMO Analytics National?

Everything in Private Lender Radar Regional, plus nationwide coverage, unlimited usage, aggregated active-loan performance trends, daily 30-, 60-, and 90-day delinquency benchmarks, risk filters, and neighborhood-level heatmaps.

Does TMO Analytics expose individual borrower or loan records?

No. TMO Analytics presents aggregated and anonymized market-level trends and risk indicators. It does not expose individual TMO loans, borrowers, or personally identifiable information.
