Use DealMachine MCP with any AI assistant

Zapier DealMachine MCP lets you connect DealMachine's actions with any AI tool that supports MCP, all without managing integrations or writing glue code. It's built for developers who want flexibility without complexity.

Step 1: Generate your secure MCP URL

Get your unique, dynamic MCP server URL instantly. This endpoint securely connects your AI assistant to Zapier's vast integration network.

Step 2: Choose your Zapier Actions

Customize the specific actions your AI assistant can perform within DealMachine. Select and scope actions to align with your DealMachine workflows.

Step 3: Connect with your tool of choice

Integrate your AI Assistant seamlessly using your generated MCP endpoint, enabling your AI Assistant to execute real-world tasks securely and reliably.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MCP?
Model Context Protocol allows your AI apps to securely interact with tools. Zapier MCP connects you to Zapier's vast ecosystem of 9,000+ integrations.
Is MCP Secure?
Yes, MCP endpoints include built-in authentication for secure, reliable connections.
How much does Zapier MCP cost?
Zapier MCP is available to all accounts. One MCP tool call uses 2 tasks from your plan's quota.
Find the right Zapier plan
Can I use Zapier MCP with any AI platform?
Zapier MCP seamlessly integrates with platforms like Cursor, Claude, or any other app that supports MCPs.
Can I build on Zapier MCP as a developer?
Absolutely! Zapier MCP lets developers bring Zapier's 30,000+ Searches and Actions into their apps.
Explore documentation
How quickly can I get started?
You can set up your MCP endpoint and start running Actions in minutes.
How can I get a higher quota?
Zapier MCP is available to all accounts. One MCP tool call uses 2 tasks from your plan's quota. You can see your task usage by logging into your Zapier MCP account, and add tasks to your plan anytime.
Explore Zapier plans
Can I use Zapier MCP with my Zapier Enterprise account?
Not out of the box. Zapier MCP doesn't currently support app and action restrictions that may be in place on an Enterprise account. If you're on an Enterprise plan and would like to use Zapier MCP, please reach out to us.
