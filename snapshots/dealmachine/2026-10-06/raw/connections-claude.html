Three quick steps

Connect Claude in three steps

Add the DealMachine MCP endpoint once, authorize your workspace, and choose the connector whenever you need property intelligence.

DealMachine MCP endpointhttps://mcp.dealmachine.com
  1. 1

    Add a custom connector

    Open Customize → Connectors in Claude, choose Add custom connector, and paste the DealMachine MCP endpoint.

  2. 2

    Sign in to DealMachine

    Click Connect, sign in to DealMachine, select your workspace, and approve access. OAuth keeps your API key out of Claude.

  3. 3

    Ask Claude to use it

    Enable DealMachine from the connector menu, then ask Claude to research an owner, build a list, or run comps.
