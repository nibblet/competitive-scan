Find Better Properties. Drive Smarter. Reach Owners Faster. DealMachine brings mobile Driving for Dollars and nationwide property intelligence together in one app. Explore properties on the map, track your drives, save opportunities as you find them, and keep everything organized for follow-up. With DealMachine, you can: - Start a Free Drive with live route and mileage tracking. Background location helps preserve your active drive when your screen locks or the app moves to the background. - Tap properties on the map to review parcel information, ownership, condition insights, sales history, taxes, mortgages, comparable properties, and more.-- - View recorded owner information and reveal available phone numbers and email addresses based on your plan. - Mark properties as Targets or Visits while driving, then add photos, notes, tags, tasks, and reminders. - Search nationwide and narrow your results using property characteristics, owner demographics, MLS activity, sale history, list membership, and other powerful filters. - Evaluate properties using satellite imagery and Google Street View when available. - Review your personal drive history, mileage, targets, and visited properties. - Sync your mobile activity with DealMachine on the web for team visibility, organization, and follow-up. Your DealMachine account also connects with advanced web tools, including more than 700 filters, 287 exportable fields, AI-powered property discovery, dialing, automated direct mail, and CRM workflows, depending on your plan. Built for real estate investors and property-focused teams across roofing, solar, and other industries, DealMachine helps turn what you see in the field into organized, actionable opportunities.
