Frequently asked questions

Answers Before You Analyze.

Everything you need to know about the trial, the math, and how BrrrrSimply fits into your day.

What makes this different from a typical real estate calculator?

Our tool is not just a calculator. It’s an entire BRRRR deal analysis program. It walks you through all stages of the plan (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat) and gives you actual numbers for cash flow, equity, and long-term return

Can it be applied to any property?

Yes. No matter if you are analyzing single-family homes, duplexes, or small multi-unit properties, the analyzer can be adapted to work on any style of residential BRRRR transaction.

What is in the premium plan?

The premium level has unlimited deal analysis, complete rehab and refinance calculators, exportable reports, and access to future feature releases. It's designed for serious real estate investors who want to grow their portfolios.

Do I receive updates with the paid version?

Yes. All premium users are rewarded with new features, improvements to tools, and a performance boost for free.

How is After Repair Value (ARV) determined in the analyzer?

User-input ARV is done manually using comparable sales or an appraiser's estimates. ARV is utilized by the analyzer to calculate the loan-to-value (LTV) percentages, cash-out refinance capacity, and total equity post-rehab.
