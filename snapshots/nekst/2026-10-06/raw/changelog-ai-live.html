Nekst AI is officially live inside your account.

This release marks a shift in how real estate transaction management software should work. Instead of manually searching through tasks, notes, and documents, you can now ask questions and get instant answers based on your actual transaction data.

Nekst AI is built directly into your workspace and understands your transactions, deadlines, comments, and documents.

What You Can Do with Nekst AI

You can now:

• Ask questions about any transaction
• Instantly view upcoming deadlines
• Search across notes and comments
• Generate closing summaries
• Review team activity
• Jump directly into tasks and transactions from chat
• Reference documents within your conversations

All AI conversations are saved so you can revisit them later. You can also create and reuse common prompts for recurring needs.

How It Works

Nekst AI lives inside your account and uses secure third-party AI providers to generate responses based on your transaction data. It does not train public models using your information.

Each plan includes a monthly AI allowance that is shared across your team. You can view your remaining usage inside your Settings page.

Why This Matters

Transaction management tools have traditionally been storage systems for tasks and documents. Nekst AI moves beyond that.

Instead of digging for information, you simply ask. Instead of clicking through multiple tabs, you get clarity in seconds.

This is the direction transaction management real estate software is heading. We are excited to build it with you.
