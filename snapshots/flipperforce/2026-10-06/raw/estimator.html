Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.
What is rehab estimating software?
Rehab estimating software helps real estate investors create detailed, itemized estimates of the repair and renovation costs on a house flip. Instead of paying a contractor to price every potential deal, you build the estimate yourself from a customizable cost database, so you know your rehab budget before you make an offer.
Can I estimate rehab costs without a contractor?
Yes. FlipperForce's rehab estimator is built specifically so you can estimate repair costs yourself, using pre-built cost databases and starter templates. You get a detailed, defensible estimate in minutes without waiting on a contractor bid.
How accurate is the rehab cost estimate?
Your estimate is as accurate as your cost database, which is why FlipperForce lets you fully customize every category, repair item, and unit price to match your market. You start from over 500 common repair items and prices, then tune them to what things actually cost in your area.
Can I create a scope of work for my contractors?
Yes. The estimator generates a detailed scope of work document with pre-written notes for each repair category, so your contractors know exactly what to include in their bids. You can share the scope of work report and track the bids that come back.
Does it work for full gut rehabs and new construction?
Yes. There are pre-built templates with pricing databases for cosmetic rehabs, gut rehabs, additions, and new home builds, so you can start fast on any project type.
How much does the rehab estimator cost?
The rehab estimator is included in FlipperForce's plans, and you can start with a free trial, no credit card required. See our pricing page for current plan details.
