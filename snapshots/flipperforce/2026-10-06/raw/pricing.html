Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.
Is FlipperForce Only For Professional House Flippers?
Absolutely not! FlipperForce is a powerful tool built for both professional house flippers and newbies. In fact, if you are a new investor FlipperForce will help you "level-up" to look like a Pro and hopefully prevent costly mistakes!
Is FlipperForce A Software Download Or A Native App?
Most of FlipperForce's software features are found in the FlipperForce web application that is accessible from your web browser, and built to be mobile responsive for use on any device.

FlipperForce has a native mobile app called FlipperForce Field that offers limited functionality for users to post Project Updates and upload Photos from their phone.
How Many Users Can Access My Workspace?
The # of users you can have in your workspace is based upon the plan you select. Our Solo plan allows one single individual to access your workspace. Our Team Plan provides access to up to 5 users and our Business plan provides unlimited user access to your workspace.
How Many Projects Can I Have In My Account?
The # of 'Active Projects' you can have in your account is based upon the plan you select. Our Solo plan allows up to 50 Active Projects, our Team up to 100, and our Business is unlimited.Note: Old projects can be archived and do not count against your Project quota.
Do You Offer Support?
Yes, we absolutely offer support!  All support inquiries are handled personally by our founder, Dave.  This means you'll receive the most knowledgeable and insightful help possible, straight from the creator of the platform.  

You can reach Dave via chat in the software or by emailing support@flipperforce.com.
What Is Your Refund Policy?
Monthly Plans (Month-to-Month)
Our monthly plans offers the most flexibility to pay on a monthly basis. If you choose to cancel your monthly plan you your account will be canceled at the end of the month's billing cycle and you will not be charged again in the future.
‍
Annual Plans (30 Day Full Money Back Guarantee)
Our annual plans offer up to a 25% discount for buying a subscription for the entire year. We also offer a 30-day full money back guarantee after you purchase an annual plan. If you choose to cancel your annual plan after this period, your account will be canceled at the end of the annual billing cycle and you will not be charged again in the future.
How Can I Cancel My Account?
We make cancelling easy. ​You can cancel your subscription yourself directly within the Billing area of the app. Or cancel by simply emailing support@flipperforce.com or chat with us directly.
What Forms Of Payment Do You Accept?
We accept all major credit cards including Visa, MasterCard, American Express & Discover Card! We do not accept ACH Payments or Check payments.
Is My Payment Information Secure?
Yes, we use enterprise SSL encryption and do not store your payment or billing information on our servers.

Don't Know Which Plan To Pick?

Start with our Solo Plan which includes our entire toolset for analyzing deals, estimating rehab costs & managing rehab projects. You can always easily change plans later!
